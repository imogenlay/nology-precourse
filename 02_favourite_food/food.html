Spaghetti Bolognese Recipe


My spaghetti! (I stole this photo from internet sorry)

This spaghetti bolognese is very easy to make and is a crowd pleaser. This is optional, but I like to add some paprika as well.

This dish usually takes about 15 minutes to prep and 30 or so minutes to cook. Total: 45 Minutes

Ingredients

  • Oil
  • Water
  • Salt and pepper
  • Spaghetti
  • Cheese

  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 brown onions, finely chopped
  • 1 grated carrot
  • 500g beef mince
  • 800g can crushed tomato
  • 2 beef bouillon cubes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tsp white sugar
  • 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 dried bay leaves
  • 2 tsp oregano
  • 2 tsp basil
  • 2 tbsp BBQ sauce

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion and garlic, cook for 3-5 minutes, until light golden and softened.
  2. Add the beef mince and cook, breaking it up as you go, until browned.
  3. Add half a cup of water. Bring to simmer and cook for 1 minute, scraping the bottom of the pot.
  4. Add remaining ingredients except salt and pepper. Stir, bring to a simmer then turn down the heat a little so it bubbles gently. Cook for 20 - 30 minutes (no lid), adding water if the sauce gets too thick. Stir occasionally. Adjust salt and pepper to taste.
  5. Serve with cheese and spaghetti!

This is the footer. This recipe can serve 8-12 people.
